# Episode Ledger

Independently identified **physical / logistics stress episodes** for the U.S. grain system.

## Identification rules

- Identify episodes from **physical and logistics evidence**, not from subsequent market moves.
- During initial identification, leave **market outcomes blank**.
- Prefer contemporaneously knowable information; record what was knowable as-of which dates.
- Do not invent sources, dates, or release delays.
- Pairwise market associations discovered later are exploratory only and do not redefine the episode.

## Four-statement reminder

When later analyzing an episode, keep separate:

1. What the data show
2. Why we think it happens
3. What we expect next
4. How it could be traded

## Episode detail template

Copy this block for each episode. Fill only what is supported by evidence.

### Episode ID

`EP-YYYY-###` (assign when recording a real episode)

### Physical event

Brief description of the physical / logistics stress (not the market narrative).

### Start date

`YYYY-MM-DD` (or best-supported range start)

### End date

`YYYY-MM-DD` (or best-supported range end; open if ongoing at identification)

### Geography

Regions, corridors, hubs, origins/destinations involved.

### Physical / logistics evidence

Observable operational facts (flows, inventories, congestion, outages, weather impacts on logistics, etc.).

### Contemporaneously knowable information

What was available to market participants / researchers **as of** which dates? Cite publication timing where known. Do not invent delays.

### Possible causes

Hypotheses only; label as such. Separate from “what the data show.”

### Data sources

Named sources and identifiers **only if known**. No fabricated source IDs.

### Notes

Caveats, open questions, links to catalog series IDs when they exist.

### Market outcomes

**Intentionally left blank during initial identification.**

Populate only in a later, explicitly authorized pass that remains labeled as outcome documentation — not as the reason the episode was selected.
