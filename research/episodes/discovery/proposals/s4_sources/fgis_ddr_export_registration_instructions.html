Exporter Registration - Who Needs to Register?

The United States Grain Standards Act (USGSA) requires anyone whose business buys, handles, weighs or transports 15,000, or more, metric tons of grain for sale in foreign commerce to be registered with the U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) Agricultural Marketing Service’s (AMS) Federal Grain Inspection Service (FGIS) before they ship grain overseas.

Those required to register are also required to provide information on any person considered to have an ownership interest of 10 percent or more in the business.  See 7.U.S.C. 87f-1.

Those who are registered by December 15 will be included in the FGIS Registered Grain Exporters Directory, which is generally released in early January.  Registrations completed after December 15 may not appear in the January release of the directory but will be added upon FGIS approval.

Special Note for Exporters of Grain to China

All grain exporters, regardless of the volume they ship, must be registered and listed on the General Administration of Customs China’s (GACC) website to export grain to China. FGIS provides the Registered Grain Exporters Directory to GACC through the USDA Foreign Agricultural Service (FAS). The directory is typically published on GACC’s website as the “List of registered companies exporting grain from the United States to China” and is commonly referred to as “the GACC List.”

The Chinese government may refuse entry to any grain shipment from an exporter that does not appear on the GACC List. Therefore, if you plan to export grain to China, please register as instructed below and be thorough and accurate in registering your export operations and locations, whether you are the main entity (person), a subsidiary, a DBA, or operating under a type of inspection and weighing waiver.

Exporting Grain to China Under Waiver

Entities planning to operate under an authorized waiver, in addition to notifying FGIS of their intent to operate under a waiver, must register with FGIS to be included on the annual listing provided to GACC.

FGIS will send quarterly grain exporter registration updates, which include changes to the Directory and Grain Exporting Locations report, to FAS after the initial annual directory is published. 

Entry Challenges

Entry challenges within China should be referred to USDA’s Foreign Agricultural Service (FAS). Please contact FASChinaDAPQRegistrations@usda.gov for assistance. For importation challenges with other foreign countries please contact FAS -Overseas Offices.

Voluntary Registration of Grain Exporters

Grain exporters who are not required to register with FGIS may still choose to do so by following the same instructions as an entity that is required to register (See “How to Register”).

Publication

FGIS will list the U.S. entities registered to buy, handle, weigh, or transport grain for sale in foreign commerce on the AMS website. The list will include entity name, business address, and phone number. Any other facilities/addresses marked for public release will be published on the Registered Grain Exporting Locations report. 

Update to application

The FGIS Delegations/Designations and Export Registrations (DDR) system no longer processes Financial Management Modernization Initiative payments for export registration; all applicants must use Pay.gov to pay the registration fee. As a reminder, FGIS has added a feature to the registration application that allows applicants to identify which locations should be published. Entities that choose to identify multiple locations in their registration submission should consider the company name(s) and address(es) that will be used on export documents to ensure they will match what is included on the FGIS Registered Grain Exporters Directory and Registered Grain Exporting Locations report.
